Financial metrics

Every metric on the Lightyear dashboard and in the Excel workbook: where it is, the formula, and how to read it.

How this is arranged. The sections follow the dashboard from top to bottom, one section per card, with the card's own name. Under each heading two lines say where to find it: the dashboard card, and the workbook sheet. Part 1 is the main display, on monthly returns. Part 2 is the daily calculations and the run diagnostics, both collapsed on the page. Part 3 is the Gate B tab.

In the workbook, a formula means Excel calculates the figure itself; a pasted value means Python calculated it and the workbook only shows it. There are no run results or examples here.

Notation used in every formula

SymbolMeaning
r, bOne day's return on the strategy and on the benchmark, the NIFTY 500 price index.
mOne calendar month's return, compounded from the daily returns. Used throughout Part 1.
Rf (5%)The risk-free rate in every Sharpe and Sortino. One config setting. A yardstick, not income.
cThe card's cash rate: what idle cash earns in the backtest. Beta and the factor regression subtract it. Not the risk-free rate.
VtPortfolio value on day t.
mean, stdevAverage and sample standard deviation (divides by n − 1). Pop stdev divides by n; Part 1 uses it.
√252, √12Annualise daily and monthly volatility. 252 is the assumed trading days a year.
yearsCalendar days ÷ 365.25. In Part 1, months ÷ 12.

Part 1. The main display (monthly)

Everything in Part 1 is on monthly returns. Calendar-month returns m, the population standard deviation, CAGR by month count, and three inputs: risk-free rate 5%, Omega threshold 1% a month, drawdown limit 0%. Complete calendar months only: a month counts when the run has a return for every trading session in it; the first month also counts when the only session without a return is its first, the run's starting point. Days outside complete months are left out and listed in the notes at the foot of the page. Python and Excel calculate each figure separately, and the sheet "Python vs Excel" marks each OK or FAIL at 1e-9. The benchmark is the NIFTY 500 price index until a total-return series is on file.

Part 3. The Gate B tab

Dashboard: Gate B tab, card "Gate B: investment decision" (gate version v4): the tables "Hard blocks" and "Evidence scorecard".
Excel: diagnostics workbook, sheet "Gate B", pasted results. The status is repeated on its "Summary" sheet.

Hard blocks decide the status, PASS or FAIL. Scorecard rows are green, amber or red and do not block. A test with nothing to run on shows as not run, which is not a pass. Every Sharpe here is arithmetic, over the risk-free rate of 5%.
