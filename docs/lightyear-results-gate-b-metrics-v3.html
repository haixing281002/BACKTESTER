Financial metrics

Every metric on the Lightyear dashboard and in the workbook: the formula, and how to read it.

Part 1 is the daily block: the tiles, charts and Core performance table Lightyear builds for every run. Part 2 is SE Return Analytics, the monthly block. Both are on the Results tab, and the workbook calculates both with live formulas.

There are no run results or examples here. The only numbers are method settings, checked against the code.

Notation used in every formula

SymbolMeaning
r, bOne day's return on the strategy and on the benchmark, the NIFTY 500 price index.
mOne calendar month's return, compounded from the daily returns. Used in Part 2.
Rf (5%)The risk-free rate in every Sharpe and Sortino. One config setting. A yardstick, not income.
cThe card's cash rate: what idle cash earns in the backtest. Beta subtracts it. Not the risk-free rate.
VtPortfolio value on day t.
mean, stdevAverage and sample standard deviation (divides by n − 1). Pop stdev divides by n; Part 2 uses it.
√252, √12Annualise daily and monthly volatility. 252 is the assumed trading days a year.
yearsCalendar days ÷ 365.25. In Part 2, months ÷ 12.

Part 1. On the Lightyear dashboard (daily)

1.1 The five headline numbers

These tiles appear on every run's Results tab, for the strategy and the benchmark over the same days. The comparators table repeats CAGR, volatility, Sharpe and worst fall for each alternative.

CAGR: the steady yearly growth rate from start to finish

CAGR=(VendVstart)1years−1

Volatility: how much returns swing in a year. Lower is a smoother ride

Vol=stdev(rdaily)×252

Sharpe (risk-free rate 5%): return above the risk-free rate, per unit of risk. Higher is better

Sharpe=CAGR−5%Vol

The 5% is the risk-free rate, a yardstick. It is not income the strategy earns: idle cash earns the card's cash rate. This headline Sharpe is the geometric form. Figures for a single calendar year use the arithmetic form shown in 1.4.

Worst fall from peak (maximum drawdown): the biggest loss from a high to a later low

Max drawdown=maxt(1−Vtmaxs≤tVs)

100 became: what 100 grew into, after every daily return

Final=100×∏t(1+rt)

Part 2. SE Return Analytics (monthly)

A separate block, on monthly returns. It is on the Results tab and in the workbook sheet "SE Return Analytics". Every figure uses calendar-month returns m compounded from the daily returns, the population standard deviation, and three inputs: risk-free rate 5%, Omega threshold 1% a month, drawdown limit 0%. Do not compare a figure here with a daily figure of the same name in Part 1. The benchmark is the price index.

Python and Excel calculate each metric separately. Python calculates it in ros/metrics/se.py; Excel calculates it from a live formula in the workbook. The workbook's "Python vs Excel" sheet sets the two side by side and marks each OK or FAIL at 1e-9.

MetricFormulaHow to read it
